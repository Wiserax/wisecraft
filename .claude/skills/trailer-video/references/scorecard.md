# Review scorecard

Score each line out of 10 from the contact sheet, the frame scans and the loudness numbers. Below 7 on
**clarity of the loop** or the **finale**: recut before sharing. Give the producer the scores with the file.

| # | Line | A 9 looks like | Usual fix when low |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | the most spectacular moment in the first 3 s, with one caption that works muted | open on the boss, not on the logo or a menu |
| 2 | Clarity of the loop | by about 8 s a viewer knows what the player does: tap → stronger → horde melts | show the finger and the upgrade; cut one spectacle shot to make room |
| 3 | One idea per shot | each shot can be named in three words | split a shot that shows two features; drop the weaker |
| 4 | Escalation and arc | each shot is bigger than the last, ending on the titan | reorder: horde → giants → reveal → towers → pierce → skill → titan |
| 5 | Pacing and rhythm | cuts on the beat within about 16 ms; shot lengths vary | snap the EDL to frames; mix 1, 2 and 4-beat shots |
| 6 | VFX polish | explosions and lightning hold up in a still frame | fix the effect in the game, then reshoot |
| 7 | Motion smoothness | `wise dupframes`: no repeats outside the end card | one sim step per frame; higher capture rate for slow motion |
| 8 | Sound design and sync | SFX under the music, impacts on the cut, about -15 LUFS, peak ≤ -1.5 dB | add the game's own SFX; duck the music 3–10 dB on impacts |
| 9 | Platform fit | vertical, titles inside the safe box for at least 0.6 s, readable on a phone | move titles; shorten to one line |
| 10 | Brand and call to action | logo + PLAY FREE held to the last frame | hold the end card while the music fades; don't fade the picture |

## Weak spots that came back from real reviews
- A creature shot small and from above reads as nothing. Reshoot close, facing the camera, filling about
  45% of the frame height.
- The hero's hit landed outside the vertical frame. Pull the camera back over the wind-up so the impact is
  in frame on the beat.
- In 16:9 the titan was seen from behind. Re-angle to its front; keep the timings.
- Titles sat on the edge of the phone panel in 16:9. Put each title inside the close-up it describes, and
  title only the first clip of a group.
- The finale celebration's fireworks were small in frame. A hero finale needs a close shot.

## What to tell the producer
- The file paths (vertical, 720p, 16:9), length, frame rate.
- The scorecard.
- Measured: duplicate frames, loudness, worst cut-to-beat error.
- "Nobody has watched it in motion or heard it; checked from frames, sheets and numbers."
