# A full run: GRAFT, a 3-day prototype (Wed → Fri)

**The input:** a concept doc (a Dota 2 Custom Hero Chaos-like hero clash), the twist "you become what you
kill", A-2 concept art, and real engine frames. Constraints: release on Friday, no real multiplayer (bots
only), wordless, web.

## v1 (the designer's first take)
- **The cycle:** 12 screens. Hatchery → hero draft → lobby of 8 totems → camp with a beetle shop → 40 s
  fight → card level-up → mutation stages → round end → boss / duel / candle lives → podium.
- **Signals:** a sun arc for the timer, a DNA ring, totems, candles, a coin pile, card pips.
- **Panel:** hardcore 5, midcore 6, casual 6, non-reader 7 /10. Publisher: green-light *with changes*.
- **What the panel found** (all were invisible to the designer):
  - the run was 11-12 min, not 6-9 (the arithmetic of rounds × camp);
  - the twist was hidden behind cards and only visible after 2 picks, while the ads and the frames sold
    "pick up the claw";
  - "stand still to shoot" failed both the casual and the melee hero;
  - the camp was LOST for both casual personas;
  - the sun arc, candles, grey totems ("locked"), card pips and coin pile all failed for the non-reader;
  - one English sentence and a "NEW" emoji had slipped into a wordless board;
  - ads that relight lives sold placement;
  - the scope could not ship by Friday.

## v2 (after the synthesis)
- **The novel mechanic moved into the first 20 s:** the DNA ring fills, then 3 real limbs rise in light
  beams; walk into one; the limb is on your body and your shots change. There's no card screen.
- **Cut to week 2:** camp/shop, duels, draft, the melee hero, stage 3, candles, meta. 8 rounds × 35 s ≈ 6 min.
- **Signals swapped for objects:** an hourglass pillar, rivals stepping out of portals into a portrait strip,
  a skull on the fallen, an ad mark the same size as ✖, a ladder of 8 for the result.

## Round 2 (the same reviewers, resumed)
- **Scores (D1):** hardcore 7, midcore 8, casual 7, non-reader 8. Publisher: **green-light**.
- **The remaining fixes were small and concrete:**
  - freeze time during the choice (dodging into the wrong beam);
  - make stage 2 a mechanic, not a skin, plus a hybrid bonus;
  - mark "me" with a gold frame everywhere;
  - draining sand in the hourglass;
  - a thick XP pie (the thin arc read as a danger ring);
  - a TV-shaped ad mark (a bare ▶ reads as "continue": an ad trap);
  - limbs flying out of the kill (the hook "rip off its claw" was only half true);
  - analytics gaps;
  - a no-graft control creative to credit any CTR gain.
- They went into v3 directly; a 3rd round wasn't needed.

## Lessons for the next run
- **The real frames beat the design doc.** The panel compared DESIGN.md with FINAL-ALL.jpg and preferred what
  the engine already showed. Always give reviewers the real frames.
- **Do the session arithmetic on the board** (rounds × (round + transition) + interrupts).
- **The ad and the first 20 s must show the same moment.**
- **Grey means "locked" to non-readers;** a skull means "out". A bare ▶ means "continue".
- **Resume the same reviewers in round 2:** they check their own issues (FIXED / PARTLY / NOT) instead of
  starting over.
- **Time:** two rounds took about 2 hours with 3 reviewer agents in parallel.
